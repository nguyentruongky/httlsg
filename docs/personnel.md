# Nhân sự Hội Thánh

The department catalog has three groups. **Ban hát lễ:** Ban Ấu nhi, Ban Thiếu nhi, Ban Thiếu niên, Ban Thanh niên, Ban Thanh tráng, Ban Tráng niên, Ban Trung niên, Ban Lão niên, Ban Phụ nữ. **Ban ngành:** Ban Âm nhạc, Ban Âm thanh - Ánh sáng, Ban Biên soạn Bài học KTHT, Ban Cầu nguyện, Ban Giữ xe, Ban Hậu cần, Ban Khánh tiết, Ban Phiên dịch, Ban Thăm viếng, Ban Tiếp tân - Trật tự, Ban Truyền giảng, Ban Truyền thông, Ban Trường Chúa nhật, Ban Tu bổ sửa chữa, Ban Y tế xã hội, Điểm nhóm Phú Lợi. **Khác:** Ban Chấp sự and Nhân viên are listed in `/dashboard/departments` but are not Ban ngành.

Age-based choir groups use Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh age on March 1: 0–6 Ấu nhi; 7–12 Thiếu nhi; 13–18 Thiếu niên; 19–30 Thanh niên; 31–40 Thanh tráng; 41–50 Tráng niên; 51–60 Trung niên; 61+ Lão niên. On March 1 each year, the scheduled job moves members with a valid date of birth between these eight groups and preserves their other department memberships. Profiles without usable birth dates are left unchanged. Admins can run the same idempotent update from **Thành viên → Thao tác → Cập nhật ban hát lễ**.

The Vercel schedule runs at 00:00 on March 1 in Vietnam time (17:00 UTC on February 28) and requires the server-only `CRON_SECRET` environment variable. Migration 025 stores department group types and seeds the canonical catalog. Matching in personnel mission selectors continues to ignore capitalization, accents, punctuation, and an optional “Ban” prefix while retaining existing database IDs.

“Cập nhật thông tin” opens the member profile. “Thay đổi công tác” opens an editable list with one mission selector and delete action per row. Ban điều hành, Ban hướng dẫn and Đặc trách require an additional Ban hát/Ban ngành selector. Trưởng ban ngành selects a Ban ngành. Ordinary choir membership is not a Công tác: it stays on the profile, is omitted from the mission popup, and is retained during mission saves. Ordinary ministry membership can be selected directly. Existing departments outside the catalog remain available for their current assignments.

The missions API replaces the submitted mission list atomically, rejects stale edits, retains historical board snapshots, and appends revisions for current-year board changes. Existing application roles remain subject to the same authorization and self-assignment restrictions. Đặc trách is stored as a church responsibility without a new application access role. Migration 023 seeds missing departments and stores these additional responsibilities.

OTP lists load on opening and through the explicit “Làm mới” action. There is no live stream, polling, window-focus refresh, or reconnect refresh. The retired `/api/leader/events` route returns 410 after authorization, and the compatibility live-sync hook opens no connections. On mobile, the hidden OTP panel does not mount or fetch until selected.

The “Thay đổi công tác” popup has its own smooth opening animation on desktop and mobile. Shared mobile select controls open a bottom sheet; desktop controls retain the native select. The All members table displays Liên hệ and Ban ngành/Ban hát lễ, revealing 20 more preloaded rows as its scroll sentinel becomes visible. Profile selection and row actions remain available inside these two columns.

Mobile selectors determine their layout synchronously and apply a selection only after their sheet has closed, avoiding a native-select-to-sheet swap and popup flashes. All members uses the same mobile card layout as Nhân sự. Search stretches beside one combined role/status filter; sorting is available through column titles rather than a separate sorting control. Specific offices (Trưởng ban, Phó ban, Thư ký, Thủ quỹ, Ủy viên) suppress the generic Ban điều hành title for the same department. Distinct specific offices and assignments in other departments remain visible.
